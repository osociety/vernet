Features

Everything you need to understand your network

No technical jargon. No confusing setup. Just clear answers about what's connected and how well it's working.

📶

Wi-Fi details

See your Wi-Fi name, BSSID, local address, gateway, and subnet at a glance.

🔍

Find devices

Scan your network to find connected devices, and optionally start a scan whenever the app opens.

🔓

Open ports scanner

Check popular ports, a custom range, or a single port on any device or website.

📡

Ping & DNS lookup

Check if a website or device responds, look up addresses, and find device names with reverse DNS.

⚡

Speed test

Measure your real download and upload speed whenever you need to.

🌐

ISP information

See your public IP and internet provider without digging through settings.

🔔

Scan notifications

Start device scans when the app opens and get notified when the results are ready.

🎛️

Scan controls

Adjust scan ranges, ping count, and connection timeouts to suit your network.

🛡️

Privacy controls

Choose whether Vernet can use internet access and location permission. Your location stays on your device.

Discover

Find every device on your network in seconds

One tap starts a scan of your local network. Vernet lists every connected device — routers, laptops, phones, smart TVs — along with their address, so you always know what's using your Wi-Fi.

  • Works over Wi-Fi, no extra setup required
  • Shows device names and network addresses
  • Spot unfamiliar devices at a glance
Investigate

Check open ports on any device or site

Curious what services a device or website exposes? Scan the top ports, a custom range, or a single port, and see clear, plain-English results — no security expertise needed.

  • Popular targets and common ports built in
  • Custom ranges for deeper checks
  • Useful findings, explained simply
Measure

Test your internet speed in seconds

Run an inbuilt speed test to see how your connection is performing, right from Vernet.

  • Check your download and upload speeds
  • Watch results update as the test runs
  • Run a fresh test whenever you need it
Check connection

Ping a website and check if it responds

Enter a website or host to test whether it is reachable and see how quickly it responds.

  • Test websites and network hosts
  • See response times for each ping
  • Spot connection delays or dropped replies
