Features

No technical jargon. No confusing setup. Just clear answers about what's connected and how well it's working.

Wi-Fi details

See the network you're connected to, its address, and signal strength at a glance.

🔍

Scan your network to find every device connected to it, from routers to phones.

Check which services are open and reachable on any device or website.

Discover

Find every device on your network in seconds

One tap starts a scan of your local network. Vernet lists every connected device — routers, laptops, phones, smart TVs — along with their address, so you always know what's using your Wi-Fi.

  • Works over Wi-Fi, no extra setup required
  • Shows device names and network addresses
  • Spot unfamiliar devices at a glance
Investigate

Check open ports on any device or site

Curious what services a device or website exposes? Scan the top ports, a custom range, or a single port, and see clear, plain-English results — no security expertise needed.

  • Popular targets and common ports built in
  • Custom ranges for deeper checks
  • Useful findings, explained simply

Free, open source, and built in the open

The full source code is public on GitHub under an open license, for anyone to read, build, or modify. The Google Play build includes ads to help fund development — Apple charges a $99/year developer fee versus Google's one-time $25, so those ads are also what will get Vernet onto the App Store. The F-Droid and GitHub builds stay ad-free. No account and no data sold, either way.
