Take control of your home or office network with Vernet — a free, open-source toolkit that puts network insights in your hands.

Whether you're troubleshooting a slow connection, checking who's on your Wi-Fi, or making sure your devices are reachable, Vernet gives you clear, easy-to-understand answers — no technical background required.

🔍 What's on my network?
See every device connected to your local Wi-Fi, along with their IP addresses and names. Know exactly who and what is sharing your connection.

📡 Is my internet working?
Run a ping test to check if a website or device is responding. Spot drops and delays in seconds.

⚡ How fast is my connection?
Test your real download and upload speed with the built-in speed test. Get accurate results without leaving the app.

🔓 What services are exposed?
Scan a device for open ports to see which services are reachable — helpful for understanding your home network's security posture.

🌐 What's behind this address?
Use built-in DNS tools to look up domain names, resolve IPs, and trace how addresses are mapped on the internet.

📊 Who is my internet provider?
View details about your ISP, including your public IP, location, and connection type.

✅ Free & open source
Vernet is completely free, contains no ads, and is open source on GitHub: https://github.com/osociety/vernet
